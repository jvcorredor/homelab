# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for agents working in this repository.

Read [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](./ARCHITECTURE.md) first — it is the live-system
map and the entry point to everything else. [`CONTEXT.md`](./CONTEXT.md)
is the canonical glossary; [`docs/adr/`](./docs/adr/README.md) holds the
decisions (ADR-0002 and ADR-0004 are in force; ADR-0009 is the current
frame).

Do not reintroduce managed addons casually: installing something by hand
and understanding it is the admission price (ADR-0009).
